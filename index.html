Your review enquiry.

The form collects your work email, company name, estimated spend range and any services you choose to list. Pare uses this information to assess pilot fit and contact you about a possible review.

Submitting the form does not connect your accounts, upload company documents or commit you to a purchase. Form submissions are processed and stored by Formspree. We use PostHog to measure page visits, review-interest clicks and successful form submissions. PostHog uses browser local storage to recognise returning browsers. We do not send your form contact details or services text to PostHog, and we use session replay to understand clicks, scrolling and navigation. Form input values are masked in recordings; network request bodies and console recording are disabled.
